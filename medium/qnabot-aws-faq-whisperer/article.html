🧐 What on Earth Is QnABot?

Think of QnABot on AWS as a serverless oracle that patiently answers customer questions 24 × 7 — whether they type, tap, or talk. Under the hood it’s an open-source combo of Amazon Lex, Lambda, DynamoDB, and friends, pre-wired by AWS so you can deploy a production-grade bot in under an hour (seriously: 30–45 min from template click to “Hello World”). (docs.aws.amazon.com)

🎯 Why Managers & Budget-Holders Should Care

⚙️ How It Works (Non-Tech & Tech Both Welcome)

  1. User Asks via web chat, Lex voice, or even an Alexa device.
  2. Lex does speech-to-text & intent wrangling.
  3. Lambda looks up the best answer in DynamoDB (your curated FAQ) or punts to Amazon Kendra / Bedrockfor generative magic. (aws.amazon.com)
  4. API Gateway delivers the answer faster than you can say “hold music.”
  5. CloudWatch Dashboards track accuracy, usage, and unanswered gaps so your content team can improve. (docs.aws.amazon.com)

(Techies: full reference architecture lives in the AWS doc set — go nuts.) (docs.aws.amazon.com)
